Help and limits

What this can and cannot do

  • sociyx removes content you posted, from accounts you log into. It works only through what each platform lets it list.
  • It does not guarantee a complete wipe. Platforms may not expose all of your history, deletions may take time to appear, and copies can exist elsewhere (caches, archives, other people's reposts or quotes, platform backups).
  • Deleting cannot be undone by this tool. Review the exact list, and start with a small selection.
  • Scanning is slow on purpose on some platforms, to look like normal use. A report covers only the items in its plan; it is not proof that nothing else remains.
  • Instagram uses an unofficial interface that conflicts with its Terms of Use and can trigger challenges or blocks. sociyx stops when that happens and never tries to get around a challenge or CAPTCHA.

How the steps fit together

  1. Add account: log in (a browser window opens on this computer).
  2. Scan: read your history into the local database. Stop it any time; it resumes.
  3. Browse and select: filter by type, text and dates, tick items, or select a whole conversation.
  4. Review: the exact items, the pace, and a typed confirmation: delete N.
  5. Run: live progress; pause or cancel. Paused jobs can be resumed from Home.
  6. Results: what was verified, what was not, and what to do next.

Keyboard

  • / focuses the search box when browsing. ? opens this page.
  • Everything is reachable with Tab, Shift+Tab, Space and Enter.
