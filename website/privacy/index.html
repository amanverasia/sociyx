Privacy

Short version: this website collects nothing, and the tool keeps your data on your computer.

This website

  • No analytics, trackers, advertising or fingerprinting.
  • No cookies, and nothing stored in your browser.
  • No forms, accounts or newsletter sign-up.
  • No third-party requests: fonts, styles and scripts are all served from this site. The only script copies the install commands to your clipboard when you press a button.

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any host, its servers handle technical request details such as your IP address in order to deliver pages and protect against abuse. We do not add any tracking on top, and we do not use that information.

Links to GitHub and the platform documentation leave this site and follow those sites' own policies. This site sends no referrer when you follow them.

The tool

  • It runs on your computer. Scanned items, plans, exports and sessions are stored in a private local folder and are never uploaded by sociyx.
  • No telemetry, no account, no hosted backend.
  • Network traffic goes only to the platforms you choose to use. Bluesky identity lookups also go to plc.directory and the public Bluesky AppView.
  • Your database and exports contain the text of your own posts. They are personal data but not credentials. The secrets folder does hold credentials, so protect it like a password.

More detail, including how to wipe local data, is in the safety page and the security policy.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue on GitHub. Do not include passwords, tokens or exports of your content.
