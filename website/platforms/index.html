Platforms

What sociyx can list and delete on each platform, and what has actually been checked.

Alpha. The only real-account results so far are for Instagram: scan, list and DM unsend, reported by the account owner. Everything else is tested against mock servers and, for Bluesky and Mastodon, live read-only requests to public data. Your first real run should be a plan of one to three items you have reviewed.

Pacing is deliberately conservative. Platforms can change their limits, and sociyx stops or waits when told to.

Bluesky

Records are listed and deleted directly in your own repository on your data server. Login is OAuth in a visible browser window on Bluesky's own page (you handle your password and 2FA, and sociyx never sees the password), or an app password typed at a hidden local prompt.

TypeWhat it is
postPosts, replies and quote posts
likeYour likes
repostYour reposts
followAccounts you follow
blockAccounts you block
list, listitemYour lists and their entries

Verified so far

  • Read-only scanning of a public account, paging through records. Checked live against public data.
  • Looking up a missing record reports it as gone. Checked live.
  • The start of the OAuth flow is accepted by Bluesky's servers. Checked live, with no login.

Not yet verified

  • A real login (OAuth or app password), and any real delete.
  • How long deletions take to show up in feeds and search.
  • Any limit on how far back your history can be listed.

Not covered

Direct messages, your profile record, media files after deletion, and copies on relays, third-party archives and other people's quotes, replies and screenshots.

After each delete, sociyx reads the record back from your data server. That is the source of truth, but feeds and search can lag behind it. Details: Bluesky notes.

Mastodon

Works against the API of your home instance. Login is OAuth with PKCE in a visible browser on your instance's own page, with a manual code fallback if your instance rejects the local redirect.

TypeWhat it isAction
postYour statuses, including replies (not boosts)Delete
boostYour boostsUnboost
favouritePosts you favouritedUnfavourite
bookmarkPosts you bookmarkedRemove bookmark

Dates for favourites and bookmarks are the date of the original post, not of when you favourited or bookmarked it, so date filters apply to that.

Pace

Mastodon limits deleting and unboosting to 30 per 30 minutes per account, so sociyx waits 60 seconds between deletes. Ten thousand posts take roughly a week. Instances can change their limits.

Verified so far

Reading public posts page by page on mastodon.social, and a missing post being reported as gone. Both live, read-only.

Not yet verified

Any real login and any real delete, unboost, unfavourite or unbookmark.

Not covered

Direct messages, media attachments, poll votes, follows, lists, filters, profile fields, and copies on other servers. Other servers receive a delete request but are not guaranteed to honour it. Details: Mastodon notes.

Instagram

Unofficial API, real risk. There is no official way to do this, so sociyx uses Instagram's private mobile API through a community library. That conflicts with Instagram's terms and can lead to challenges, temporary blocks, or in the worst case a disabled account. Slow pacing lowers the chance but does not remove it. Acting on your own data does not by itself make automation permitted.

Login opens a visible browser where you log in. sociyx never types into the page and never touches CAPTCHAs. A password method and an existing session file are also available.

No API lists your comments on other people's posts. You request your Instagram data export, import it, and sociyx tries to locate each comment. Some may need manual deletion.

Pace

30 to 45 seconds between writes, a one to three minute pause every 25, and a cap of 200 per day. A 1,000-item cleanup takes ten hours or more. If Instagram signals an action block, the job stops with a 24 hour cool-down.

Not covered

Message requests, story replies, likes on comments, tags, replies to comments, follows, and any backups Instagram keeps. DM unsend removes the message, but the other person may already have seen it, been notified, or exported it. Details: Instagram notes.

Researched, not built

These have been looked into. None is implemented, and there is no promised order or date.
